Integrated modelingAgent pick

Hybrid causal simulation

Combines dynamic feedback loops, correlated uncertainty, and scenario sampling. Best when financial and operational decisions affect one another over time.

ProbabilisticAgent pick

Correlated Monte Carlo

Models joint uncertainty with correlated inputs, nonlinear KPI responses, and sensitivity analysis. Best for quantifying risk, outcome ranges, and target probabilities.

Time-dependentAgent pick

System Dynamics

Uses stock-and-flow equations, feedback, and delayed effects. Best for capacity, inventory, workforce, and compounding operational trade-offs.

Sequential decisionsAgent pick

Markov Decision Process

Optimizes actions across uncertain transitions, with explicit rewards and constraints. Best for resource allocation and decisions that adapt across multiple stages.
